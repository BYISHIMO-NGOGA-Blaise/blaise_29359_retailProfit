# Phase 0 presentation outline

Proposed 10–12 slide structure for presenting the project, from its motivation and research gap to the planned database, implementation approach, and expected results.

1. **Title** — Project title, student name and ID, course, instructor, and GitHub link.
2. **Background and problem** — Why retailers need to monitor sales and gross profitability across branches and product lines.
3. **Motivation** — Why this problem is relevant and why transaction-level analysis is useful.
4. **Objectives** — One main objective and at least three specific objectives from the proposal.
5. **Research and gap** — Findings from the two research papers and how the project differs from existing analytics systems.
6. **Data source** — Kaggle link and Apache 2.0 license; downloaded CSV (140,285 bytes, 1,000 rows, and 17 fields); dataset fields and sample records.
7. **Proposed solution and innovation** — Oracle 21c, PL/SQL, and a Power BI dashboard connected to Oracle.
8. **Planned database** — Planned tables and relationships: branch/product/payment/customer-type dimensions, sale, sale line, and audit log.
9. **PL/SQL approach** — Where procedures, functions, packages, triggers, cursors, and exceptions will be used.
10. **Expected results** — Validated sales data and interactive comparisons of sales and gross-income amounts.
11. **Limitations and future work** — Small Myanmar sample; no stock-on-hand or explicit discount fields; possible future use of a richer local dataset.
12. **References** — APA references from the [research log](../research/research_log.md).
